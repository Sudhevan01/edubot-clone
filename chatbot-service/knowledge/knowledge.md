# Documentation Portal
Complete guide to accessing and managing the EduCrop platform modules.

### Organization Overview
EduCrop is designed to power the entire operational infrastructure of your institution. Discover the working principles of our three core modules.

- Academic Management System (AMS): Centralizes student data, curriculum tracking, and performance metrics. AMS automates attendance logging, grades computation, and assignment distribution, ensuring teachers spend less time on paperwork and more time teaching.
- Receipt Management System (RMS): Acts as the financial backbone of the institution. RMS completely automates fee collections, invoice generation, and staff payroll, maintaining an immutable ledger of transactions for flawless auditing and financial transparency.
- Visitor Management System (VMS): Secures the campus perimeter. VMS leverages instant ID scanning and QR codes to digitally log every entry and exit. It cross-references watchlists and sends real-time SMS alerts to hosts, replacing unsecure paper logbooks.

### Teacher Portal & FAQs
Everything you need to know about managing your classroom, grading, and assignments.

- How do I mark daily attendance for my class? Navigate to the AMS module, select your assigned class under 'My Classes', and click on 'Mark Attendance'. You can mark students as present, absent, or on leave with a single click.
- Can I upload assignments and study materials? Yes, under the 'Assignments' tab in AMS, you can upload PDFs, documents, and external links. Students and parents will receive instant notifications when new material is posted.
- How do I enter exam grades? Go to 'Results & Examinations', select the exam term and subject, and enter the marks. The system will automatically compute grades, percentiles, and generate digital report cards.
- How can I send announcements to my class? You can send important updates and notifications directly to your class through the 'Announcements' module. These messages will be broadcasted instantly to the dashboards of your students and their parents.
- Can I register a new student to my class? Yes, as a designated class teacher, you have exclusive access to the 'Add Student' form for your specific section. This allows you to directly register new students and maintain an accurate class roster.

### Parent Portal
Stay connected with your child's academic journey. Find answers to common questions below.

- How to Register As a Parent:
  1. Scan QR Code: Scan the QR code provided by the school to download the EduCrop app.
  2. Open the App: Open the EduCrop app on your mobile device. An install popup will appear. Tap "How to Install".
  3. Tap on Register Here: On the login screen, click on "Register here" at the bottom right.
  4. Click As a Parent: Select "As a Parent" to register using your pre-registered mobile number.
  5. Fill Registration Form: Enter your registered mobile number, email address and set a new password.
  6. Send Verification Code: A code will be sent to your email. Verify to complete registration. Once verified, you can log in!
- Why is my mobile number not recognized during registration? Your mobile number must match the exact number provided to the school during admission. Contact the school administration to update your phone number in their records.
- How do I view my child's report card? Once logged in, navigate to the 'Academic Profile' tab and select 'Results' to view and download the latest report cards and progress reports.
- Can I pay school fees through the app? Yes! Go to the 'Fee Management' section to view pending dues and securely pay via credit card, UPI, or net banking. Receipts are generated instantly.
- How do I apply for leave for my child? Under the 'Attendance' section, select 'Apply for Leave', choose the dates, and provide a valid reason. The class teacher will approve or reject the request.

### Install EduCrop on iPhone (iOS App)
Since EduCrop is built as a highly optimized Progressive Web App (PWA), you can install it directly to your iPhone without needing the App Store.

- Step 1: Open Safari on your iPhone and navigate to org.educrop.com.
- Step 2: At the bottom of the Safari screen, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up). Then scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
- Step 3: The EduCrop app icon will now appear on your home screen! Tap it to launch the app in full-screen mode just like a native iOS application.
